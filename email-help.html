How to turn on email

Lucid is fully ready to send emails. It just needs one "mail box key" from you — a Gmail app password. It takes about 2 minutes, once.

Step 1 — Create a Gmail for Lucid (if you don't have one)

Any Gmail works. You can use your own, or make a brand-new free one so Lucid doesn't use your personal inbox:

Create a Gmail

Step 2 — Turn on 2-Step Verification

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com and sign in with that Gmail
  2. Click Security (left menu)
  3. Under "How you sign in with Google", click 2-Step Verification
  4. Click Get started and follow the steps (phone code)

Google requires this before it hands out app passwords. You only do it once.

Step 3 — Create the app password

  1. Back in Security, search for App passwords
  2. Select app: Mail — Select device: Other, type lucid
  3. Click Generate

Google shows 16 characters like: abcd efgh ijkl mnop

Step 4 — Give the key to Lucid

Open build-lab/email_config.py and fill in two lines: 

SENDER_EMAIL = "yourname@gmail.com"
SENDER_PASSWORD = "abcd efgh ijkl mnop"

Save the file. Then refresh the Watch area — the banner disappears and the Outbox emails change to "Sent".

This file is private to you (gitignored) — it is never uploaded to GitHub.

Still stuck? Ask your teacher or a friend for help with the Google steps — everything else is already done.
